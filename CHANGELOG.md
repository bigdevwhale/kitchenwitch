## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-11

The first release of Kitchen Witch.

- A kitchen window onto a pine forest under the real moon and your town's weather: fog, fireflies, rain and snow; a cauldron that steams on the sill.
- The moon keeps the rhythm: set one new dish as the spell of each moon; the full moon is for guests, the waning moon for leftovers.
- The bones pick a dish you've never cooked (in season first, or only from what's in your pantry), three throws a day.
- Cooking mode: an intention, steps one at a time, candle timers read from the step text, the screen stays on, then how it went, a note, guests and a photo of the plate.
- The book of shadows: every dish with its photo, note and a sigil drawn from its reagents; scrolls (recipes waiting to be cooked) and misfires.
- Write recipes down from the library, a link (schema.org recipe markup), a photo of a page, your own words, or from memory after cooking.
- The shelf: 42 jars with old kitchen beliefs; a new spice in a spell puts a new jar on it. The wheel of the year: eight feasts with their menus. 22 hidden witch's marks.
- A library of 59 recipes in English and Russian. First-visit hints on every screen. No ads, no account; only the town's coordinates go out, for the weather.
