# Privacy

Kitchen Witch has no account and no analytics. Your recipes, the book, the shelf and the photos of your plates stay on your phone, in the app's own storage. Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on.

Two things go over the network, and only these:

- **The weather for the window.** The coordinates of the town you chose (or the one guessed from your time zone) are sent to Open-Meteo, or to MET Norway when Open-Meteo can't be reached. Nothing else goes with them.
- **A recipe link you open.** When you read a recipe from a link, the app downloads that page and nothing more.

Plate and page photos are taken by your phone's own camera app and kept inside Kitchen Witch. The app doesn't ask for the camera permission.
